AI ya Asli?

10 quick rounds: did AI make it, or a human? Solo, on the big screen, or a 1v1 duel with a friend.

Purpose: everything a student staff member at counter 7 needs: the pitch, setup, the duel, the 5 common problems, privacy lines.

Needed from Avi: nothing for this card.

30-second pitch (say it your way)

"AI ne banaya ya asli hai? 10 rounds, 60 second, tum vs Detector Agent. Jitna tez, utne zyada points, aur aaj ke top 10 mein naam! Dost ke saath ho? Duel karo, TV pe dono ka score live."

What the visitor does

  1. Scans the counter 7 QR. Picks 1 of 3 generated nicknames (no typing, "Naye naam" for more).
  2. Taps Akele khelo, Big screen pe khelo, Dost ke saath duel, or Duel code hai? Join karo.
  3. 10 rounds of text and code (voice rounds only once real student clips are approved). Tap AI or ASLI; faster answers earn a speed bonus. The Detector Agent guesses too.
  4. Follow gate, then the score out of 10, points, today's rank, share card.

Duel (2 phones)

  1. Player 1 taps "Dost ke saath duel": a big 4-letter code and a QR appear.
  2. Player 2 scans that QR, or taps "Duel code hai? Join karo" and types the 4 letters.
  3. 4 s countdown, then the same 10 rounds for both. "Duel big screen pe dikhao" puts both live scores on the TV.
  4. Winner: more right answers, then faster time. Winner card: "Maine [friend's nickname] ko AI ya Asli mein haraya".

Privacy lines you must say

  • "TV aur leaderboard pe sirf tumhara game naam aata hai, asli naam kabhi nahi."
  • "Koi data save nahi hota, sirf score aur nickname, aaj ke liye."

Camera rule for under-18 visitors (filming crew): only hands and screens on camera. Palm photos (counters 1 and 8) are never stored; reels (counter 6) are deleted after 48 hours.

Setup (once, before the stall opens)

  1. Pair the TV browser first, in normal Chrome (kiosk has no address bar): open play.zenithschool.ai/staff and type the 6-digit code the lead makes with "Pair a staff device" on /admin (10 min, one device). Once per TV browser, before visitors arrive; it lasts 4 days.
  2. Then open the TV address full screen (kiosk): play.zenithschool.ai/tv/asli?c=7 (idle: "Aaj ke top 10" and a crowd round). Press H: no "Staff TV nahi" message means pairing worked.
  3. Counter phones (2, for duels with visitors): pair each the same way, one code per device.
  4. Test once: one solo game and one duel between the two counter phones.
  5. Filming crew only: phone /asli/?c=7&demo=hero, TV /tv/asli?c=7&capture=1&board=demo (fictional demo board). Film when the counter is quiet: the hero run uses counter 7's TV.

5 most likely problems

What you seeWhat to do
1The friend cannot join the duel.Scan the QR on player 1's screen, or check the 4 letters (no 0, O, 1, I or L). A room waits 10 min, then closes: start a new one.
2"[Friend] beech mein chala gaya, duel tumhara".One phone locked or left for more than 60 s: walkover. Ask both players to keep the screen on until the end.
3Two groups want the TV at once.Use "Big screen pe khelo" (solo) or "Duel big screen pe dikhao" (duel): they take turns from the queue. The operator taps Next (Stall Runbook).
4A round has audio but nothing plays.Phone volume up, silent switch off. Voice rounds appear only once real student clips are approved, so most games are text and code.
5"Line mein ho", a countdown, or a "try again" message.Busy or a network blip. The phone retries by itself; if a game is lost, start again (a second game on the same phone shows its score right away).

The board is per day (IST) and resets at midnight. Equal scores rank by faster time.
