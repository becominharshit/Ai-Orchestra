AI Jyotish For fun only

Palm + date of birth, 6 AI agents, a fun reading. Plus "Dost Compatibility" for 2 to 4 friends.

Purpose: everything a student staff member at counter 1 or 8 needs: the pitch, setup, the 5 common problems, and what you must say about privacy.

Needed from Avi: nothing for this card. Stall-wide inputs (contacts, reward item) are in the Stall Runbook.

30-second pitch (say it your way)

"Haath dikhao, AI batayega tum kaun ho! 6 AI agents tumhari palm aur date of birth padhte hain, aur TV pe live dikhta hai kaun kya kar raha hai. Sirf maze ke liye hai, photo save nahi hoti. Squad ke saath ho? Dost Compatibility try karo, 2 se 4 dost ek phone pe."

What the visitor does

  1. Scans the standee QR (counter 1 or 8) on their own phone.
  2. Types first name, date of birth, birth place (time is optional). These stay on the phone; the TV never shows them.
  3. Takes a palm photo, or taps "Photo ke bina chalao (demo haath)".
  4. Watches 6 agents work (Jyotish Guru, Rashi, Ank, Hast Rekha, Kaal, Career). The TV mirrors it if "Big screen pe dikhao" is on.
  5. Gets the reading: title, "Tum kaun ho", past. The future and the share card unlock after "Maine follow kar liya".
  6. "Doston ko bhejo" shares the card. Under-18 visitors see "Dosti" instead of "Dating".

Dost Compatibility (link under the form)

2 to 4 friends on one phone: first name (phone only) and DOB each, palm photo optional. They get a squad energy, a role per friend ("The Explorer"), and on the TV only generated nicknames like "TurboJugnu". "Big screen pe squad report" puts them on the TV and the board "Aaj ki green-flag squads". Friendship only, never romance.

Privacy lines you must say

  • "Palm ki photo sirf is reading ke liye jaati hai. Save nahi hoti, log mein nahi likhi jaati."
  • "Naam, DOB aur jagah TV pe kabhi nahi aate. TV pe sirf agents aur result."
  • "Sirf maze ke liye hai, sach ka daawa nahi."

Camera rule for under-18 visitors (filming crew): only hands and screens on camera, never faces. Reels at counter 6 are deleted after 48 hours.

Setup (once, before the stall opens)

  1. Pair the TV browser first, in normal Chrome (kiosk has no address bar): open play.zenithschool.ai/staff and type the 6-digit code the lead makes with "Pair a staff device" on /admin (10 min, one device). Once per TV browser, before visitors arrive; it lasts 4 days.
  2. Then open the TV address full screen (kiosk): counter 1 play.zenithschool.ai/tv/jyotish?c=1, counter 8 .../tv/jyotish?c=8. Press H: no "Staff TV nahi" message means pairing worked.
  3. Counter phone: pair it the same way with its own code (then no rate limit for demos).
  4. Test once: scan the standee QR, "Photo ke bina chalao", check the TV shows the agents and the reading.
  5. Put the counter where there is good light, so palm photos are clear.
  6. Filming crew only: phone /jyotish/?c=8&demo=hero, TV /tv/jyotish?c=8&capture=1&layout=portrait. The demo profile is fictional.

5 most likely problems

What you seeWhat to do
1Camera does not open, photo is blurry, or the visitor does not want a photo.Tap "Photo ke bina chalao (demo haath)". The reading still works. For a clear photo: good light, palm facing the camera, fingers up, fill the frame.
2The TV does not show this visitor.Check "Big screen pe dikhao (Counter N)" is on, and the counter number matches the TV (1 or 2). Someone may have a big-screen turn or a pin: the TV takes the next visitor after that.
3"Line mein ho" or a countdown on the phone.The AI is busy. The phone retries by itself, so just wait. It is not an error.
4TV stuck on an old reading.It goes back to the QR 22 s after the end. To clear it now: press B on the staff TV.
5A calm support card ("Tum akele nahi ho ... 14416") appears, or the amber dot on the alerts view.Follow the care steps below. No game, nothing on the TV: that is by design.

Also normal: the result says "Offline mode: ready-made reading". The live AI is off (no key, spend cap or offline). The reading is still personal by sign and number. Tell the lead once.

If a visitor sees the support card

Go over kindly, without fuss. Ask "Sab theek hai? Baat karni ho toh hum yahin hain." Tele-MANAS is 14416 (free, 24x7); the card has a call button. Never take a photo or ask for a name. Tell the shift's safety lead afterwards (time only). Never discuss it in front of the crowd.

Never say: any prediction about exams, health, marriage or money; anything negative about the visitor. If asked "Kya yeh sach hai?": "Sirf maze ke liye hai, AI ka demo hai."
Who built it (only this line): "Zenith first-years ne AI agents ke saath banaye". Programme: "B.Tech in AI + mini-MBA. Ek degree, KRMU ki." More questions: point them to the follow QR on the standee (instagram.com/zenith_school_of_ai).
