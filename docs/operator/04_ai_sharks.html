AI Sharks For fun only

A 4-card startup pitch to 3 fictional AI sharks. Deal ya out, plus a real mini-MBA lesson.

Purpose: everything a student staff member at counter 3 needs: the pitch, setup, the 5 common problems, and the chips rule when the live AI check is off.

Needed from Avi: nothing for this card. The reward item for big-screen players is a stall-wide decision (Stall Runbook).

30-second pitch (say it your way)

"Apna startup idea 4 cards mein pitch karo: idea, customer, problem, paisa. 3 AI sharks ek ek sawaal poochenge, phir deal ya out! 'Idea type chuno' se ready jawab chuno, ek bhi word type nahi karna. Asli paisa nahi, sirf maze ke liye, par lesson asli mini-MBA wala."

What the visitor does

  1. Scans the counter 3 QR. Chooses Solo or Co-founders (2 dost), optional nickname, optional "Big screen pe khelo".
  2. Fills 4 pitch cards, 15 s each: Idea, Customer, Problem, Paisa. With "Idea type chuno" chips (12 idea types, 3 ready answers per card). Voice and typing work in live mode only.
  3. Reviews the cards, then the agents work: Host, Safety Agent, the 3 sharks, Score Agent, Lesson Agent.
  4. Each shark asks one follow-up (10 s): answer by chip (voice or text in live mode only).
  5. Follow gate, then the scorecard: 4 axes out of 10, total out of 40, DEAL or "Main out hoon" per shark, a mini-MBA lesson, today's rank, share card.

The sharks: Seth Dhanraj ("Margin kitna hai, beta?"), Riya Rao ("Insta pe bikega?"), Kabir 'Scale' Mehta ("10x kaise hoga?"), and sometimes Mummy Angel Investor. All fictional characters, not real people.

Privacy lines you must say

  • "Tumhare words TV pe kabhi nahi aate. TV pe sirf sharks, score aur nickname."
  • "Sharks fictional hain, asli paisa nahi. Sirf maze ke liye."
  • "Naam ya phone number pitch mein mat bolo."

Camera rule for under-18 visitors (filming crew): only hands and screens on camera. Palm photos (counters 1 and 8) are never stored; reels (counter 6) are deleted after 48 hours.

Chips rule. When the live AI check is off ("degraded" mode, the default until the AI key is live), the game is chips only: the first button is "Type karna nahi? Idea type chuno", and typed or spoken cards and answers are refused. Guide every visitor to the chips. In live mode, voice comes first and typing works too.

Setup (once, before the stall opens)

  1. Pair the TV browser first, in normal Chrome (kiosk has no address bar): open play.zenithschool.ai/staff and type the 6-digit code the lead makes with "Pair a staff device" on /admin (10 min, one device). Once per TV browser, before visitors arrive; it lasts 4 days.
  2. Then open the TV address full screen (kiosk): play.zenithschool.ai/tv/shark?c=3. Press H: no "Staff TV nahi" message means pairing worked.
  3. Counter phone: pair it the same way with its own code.
  4. Check the mode: play.zenithschool.ai/status.json shows "safetyMode":"live" or "degraded". Degraded means chips only; live means voice and typing work too.
  5. Test once: one chip-only game, check the TV shows the tank and the verdict.
  6. Filming crew only: phone /shark/v2/?c=3&demo=hero, TV /tv/shark?c=3&capture=1 (add &layout=portrait for 9:16). R replays.

If a visitor sees the support card

Go over kindly, without fuss: "Sab theek hai? Baat karni ho toh hum yahin hain." Tele-MANAS is 14416 (free, 24x7); the card has a call button. Never take a photo or ask for a name. Tell the shift's safety lead afterwards (time only).

Never say: that an idea is bad or stupid. Every shark line is positive and fixable. Who built it (only this line): "Zenith first-years ne AI agents ke saath banaye". Programme: "B.Tech in AI + mini-MBA. Ek degree, KRMU ki."
