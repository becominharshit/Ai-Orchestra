Career Studio

Job Time Machine (2026 vs 2030) and a Career Forecast preview with sources. Optional Forecast PDF on WhatsApp.

Purpose: everything a student staff member at counter 5 needs: the pitch, setup, the 5 common problems, privacy lines, and the WhatsApp rules.

Needed from Avi: the go-ahead to switch WhatsApp live sends ON (until then it stays OFF, see page 2).

30-second pitch (say it your way)

"Tumhara dream job 2030 mein kaisa dikhega? Pehle aaj ke tareeke se 3 kaam khud karo, phir dekho AI agents ki team wahi din kaise chalati hai. Har number ka source hai, aur koi guarantee nahi, sirf ek forecast. 2 minute lagte hain."

What the visitor does

  1. Scans the counter 5 QR. Picks Job Time Machine or Career Forecast preview.
  2. Job Time Machine: picks 1 of 6 jobs (Marketing Manager, Financial Analyst, Product Manager, Software Engineer, Content Creator, Doctor).
  3. Round 1 (2026): does 3 small tasks by hand, timed.
  4. Follow gate, then Round 2 (2030): an AI agent team does the same day, and the visitor makes 2 "human calls".
  5. Result: 2026 vs 2030, work split (done by AI, done with AI, led by people), 3 skills that hold, with the source year on every number.
  6. Forecast preview: picks an interest area, sees top careers with the 2030 work split. Optional: "WhatsApp pe chahiye?" for the Forecast PDF.
  7. "Doston ko bhejo" shares the card.

Privacy lines you must say

  • "WhatsApp bilkul optional hai. Number sirf PDF bhejne ke liye, encrypted rehta hai."
  • "Message band karne ke liye kabhi bhi STOP likho, ya play.zenithschool.ai/stop."
  • "Naam ya number TV pe kabhi nahi aata."

Camera rule for under-18 visitors (filming crew): only hands and screens on camera. Palm photos (counters 1 and 8) are never stored; reels (counter 6) are deleted after 48 hours.

Never say: job, placement, salary or admission guarantees. If asked: "Yeh forecast hai, guarantee nahi." Do not quote earnings numbers. Doctor path: "AI assist karta hai, doctors decide karte hain", no medical advice.

Setup (once, before the stall opens)

  1. Pair the TV browser first, in normal Chrome (kiosk has no address bar): open play.zenithschool.ai/staff and type the 6-digit code the lead makes with "Pair a staff device" on /admin (10 min, one device). Once per TV browser, before visitors arrive; it lasts 4 days.
  2. Then open the TV address full screen (kiosk): play.zenithschool.ai/tv/career?c=5. Press H: no "Staff TV nahi" message means pairing worked.
  3. Counter phone: pair it the same way with its own code.
  4. Counter laptop: start the full Career Forecast as in experiences/career/COUNTER_LAPTOP.md (127.0.0.1:8787, "/new" for each new visitor).
  5. Test once: one Job Time Machine round, check the TV shows the agents and the result.
  6. Filming crew only: phone /career/?c=5&demo=hero plays by itself.

WhatsApp rules (staff-only switch)

  • The live-send switch is on the staff page /career/staff/ (needs the admin key). It stays OFF until the lead says. Never turn it on yourself, never change the caps there.
  • With the switch OFF the form still works: the request is saved (encrypted) and the phone says the PDF comes within 48 ghante.
  • The visitor types their own number and ticks the consent box themselves. Never type a visitor's number for them, never note it down, never photograph a screen with a name.
  • STOP: they reply STOP on WhatsApp, or open play.zenithschool.ai/stop. If they ask at the counter, help them do it on their own phone.

Go over kindly, without fuss: "Sab theek hai? Baat karni ho toh hum yahin hain." Tele-MANAS is 14416 (free, 24x7); the card has a call button. Never take a photo or ask for a name. Tell the shift's safety lead afterwards (time only).
