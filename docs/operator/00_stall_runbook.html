Stall runbook · page 2 · opening routine

Kit list (counter map v4)

8 TVs + 8 laptops (one pair per counter 1 to 8, each laptop drives its counter TV), plus an optional spare screen for the Live Projects loop slide on the side wall (print/out/pdf/slide_WALL-live-projects_16x9_1920x1080.png, full screen). Career Studio keeps its extra counter laptop for the full Career Forecast. Two 4G hotspots, chargers and extension boards, staff phones. Print: 8 standees, 16 counter tents, 8 A3 posters, 6 fallback tents (counters 2 and 4), Live Projects wall posters (PRINT_SPEC.pdf). (Laptop-per-TV split is the lead's v5 brief.)

Opening routine (every morning, before visitors)

  1. Power and network. TVs, the 8 counter laptops and phones on charge. Venue Wi-Fi or the stall hotspots on (page 5).
  2. Lead makes pairing codes. On the lead's own device, open /admin and tap "Pair a staff device": it shows a 6-digit code, valid 10 minutes, for one device only. Make one code per device.
  3. Pair each TV browser first, in normal Chrome (kiosk mode has no address bar). Open /staff and type the code. The page says "Staff device ready". That browser is now a staff browser for 4 days; /staff/off ends it. Pair again after clearing cookies, in another browser, and on the offline laptop address (pairing is per address).
  4. Then open the TV address full screen. On a Mac TV machine: open -na "Google Chrome" --args --kiosk "<TV address>" (Cmd+Q leaves kiosk). Press H. If the top says "Staff TV nahi", leave kiosk with Cmd+Q and pair again (step 3).
  5. Staff, filming and safety-lead phones: pair each one the same way (/staff, its own code). Then demos, hero runs and staff controls are not rate-limited. Never pair a visitor's phone.
  6. Check /status.json on a staff phone (table on page 4): "ok":true, reelWorker ok, and note safetyMode.
  7. One test per counter (the counter cards say how). A ?demo=hero run uses a fictional profile and is not counted.
  8. The safety lead opens /admin/alerts on their paired phone and turns "Sound + vibration" on (page 3).

Only the lead opens /admin, to make codes. Never write down or show the admin key: not on a TV, in a photo, in a chat or on paper. Never post codes in a group chat. If the key leaks, tell the lead at once (Avi changes it).

Stall runbook · page 3 · alerts view, operator view, big screen

The safety lead and the alerts view

Each shift names one safety lead. They keep the alerts view, /admin/alerts, open all shift on their paired phone, with "Sound + vibration" on, and go to the counter when an alert comes in (page 4). Counter staff can always tell the safety lead directly too.

The alerts view lists every counter's alerts. Each one shows only the time, counter, experience and kind ("Support card dikha" or "Filter ne ek gaali roki"), never anything about the visitor. "Theek hai" dismisses an alert once it is handled.

Operator view: /tv/<exp>?c=<n>&op=1

One counter, e.g. /tv/shark?c=3&op=1, on a paired phone (it shows only the panel) or a laptop. It shows that counter's alerts too. Never open the operator view or the alerts view on the public TV.

The operator view has the big screen queue (nicknames only) with Next (end the running turn, start the next), Skip (drop the first person waiting, e.g. they left), Clear (empty the queue, end the turn, drop a pending guest star) and Pin; Guest star turn (name list + "consent confirmed by staff" tick), Mark next turn as record, Hide anchor card once a card exists; and the reward token field with Reward given.

Big-screen turns

  1. Visitor taps "Big screen pe khelo" and picks 1 of 3 generated nicknames (no typing).
  2. Phone shows "Line mein ho: N aage". When the TV is free the turn starts by itself and the TV shows "BIG SCREEN: <nickname>".
  3. Up to 10 wait per counter. A waiting phone that goes away drops out after 3 min; a turn ends after 4 min at most.
  4. Other visitors still play on their phones; their TV mirror waits.

Guest star (anchor) turns

  1. Only if the anchor really plays and agrees, and the filming crew has the organisers' and anchor's OK.
  2. Anchor joins the queue with "Big screen pe khelo" (own phone or a staff phone).
  3. Pick the name (Ayushman Pandita, Gaurav Taneja, Manika Vishwakarma), tick consent, tap Guest star turn, then Next.
  4. Their result is pinned as "Beat [name] ka score". Hide: "Hide anchor card" or A.

A pending guest star waits for Next (5 min, then it expires; Clear drops it). No anchor: Mark next turn as record, then Next: "Zenith ka record". Never make up a reading or score for a real person.

Reward token

  1. After a big-screen turn of 20 s or more, the phone shows a 4-character code and QR: "Counter pe dikhao".
  2. Type the 4 characters in the token field, tap Reward given. It is counted (count only), and the code cannot be used again.
  3. A code works once, on its own counter, for 30 min.

Student feedback: if a student team asks for a change, tell the lead; requests are logged in OneDrive 03 Student Builds/Build_Review_Log.xlsx.

Stall runbook · page 4 · safety

Safety (read this before your first shift)

Care alert: a visitor saw the support card

  • If a visitor types something that sounds like self-harm or crisis, their phone shows a calm card: "Tum akele nahi ho. Kisi bade se ya kisi apne se baat karo. Tele-MANAS (free, 24x7): 14416", with a call button. There is no game or score, and nothing goes to the TV.
  • The alerts view (/admin/alerts) shows "Support card dikha" with the time, counter and experience only; the operator view shows an amber dot. The safety lead goes; counter staff can go first.
  • Go over kindly, without fuss. "Sab theek hai? Baat karni ho toh hum yahin hain." Offer water and a quiet spot. Listen; do not lecture.
  • Tele-MANAS is 14416 (free, 24x7). If someone is in immediate danger: venue security / first aid (page 6) and 112.
  • Never take a photo, a name or a number. Never discuss it in front of others or on camera. Tap "Theek hai" on the alert, then tell the safety lead (time only).

Something wrong on a TV

Press B at once (staff TV), then tell the lead. The lead can also use "Clear counter N" on /admin. Every TV text comes from reviewed lines, and visitor typing never reaches a TV, so this should be rare. Log it (page 6).

Privacy lines for every counter

  • Palm photos are not stored or logged.
  • Reels are deleted after 48 hours.
  • Names, birth dates, numbers and typed words never go on a TV.
  • Minors on our camera: hands and screens only, never faces.

Degraded mode: the live AI check is off

When: no AI key yet, the AI is down, the spend cap is reached, or the stall runs offline.

What changes: the apps keep working on reviewed lines. The care line shows more prominently. Every rule hit (care or bad language) raises the operator alert.

What staff do:

  • AI Sharks: chips only. Typing and voice are refused in degraded mode; guide visitors to "Type karna nahi? Idea type chuno".
  • The safety lead keeps the alerts view open.
  • Tell the lead. Log it as degraded, not down.

Stall runbook · page 5 · offline, spend cap, consent switches

Offline mode on the counter laptop

Use it when play.zenithschool.ai is slow or down, or the venue network fails. One laptop runs everything; TVs and staff phones join the stall hotspot. The app blocks every outbound request and uses reviewed ready-made answers. Lead or tech on-call starts it.

Start (about 5 minutes)

  1. Laptop on power, joined to the stall hotspot.
  2. Find its address: ipconfig getifaddr en0 (looks like 172.20.10.2). Write it on the counter cards.
  3. Keep it awake: caffeinate -dis & (lid open, on power).
  4. cd ~/"Documents/Zenith School of AI/Ideas 4.0 builds/play-zenith", then PUBLIC_URL=http://<address>:8790 npm run offline. The first line says mode: OFFLINE. If macOS asks to allow incoming connections for node: Allow.
  5. From a phone on the hotspot: http://<address>:8790/healthz shows {"ok":true}.
  6. TVs: http://<address>:8790/tv/<exp>?c=<n>. Pair each TV and staff phone again at this address (/staff, in normal Chrome before kiosk), with codes the lead makes on the laptop's /admin ("Pair a staff device").

While offline, and going back

  • Visitors can play only if they join the hotspot; otherwise use the staff phones at each counter. The TV QR points to the laptop.
  • Squad Reel: the reel studio starts inside the app on the laptop with the first reel, so the first one may be slower.
  • Back online: Ctrl+C in the app's Terminal tab (counts are saved), switch TVs back to the play.zenithschool.ai addresses, pair again there if the TV says "Staff TV nahi".
  • Offline counts stay on the laptop: npm run stats, add them to the day by hand.

Hotspots

A phone hotspot takes about 10 devices. With 8 TVs plus staff phones, use a second hotspot for the phones, or a small travel router. (Inference, not measured at the venue.)

Spend cap

The AI spend has a cap: 350 USD on the live site (SPEND_CAP_USD, ops/aws/environments.json). When it is reached, /status.json shows "claude":"spend_cap" and the lead's /admin page shows "mode spend_cap" with a full spend bar. Phones keep working with ready-made answers ("Offline mode: ready-made ..."), and safety turns degraded (page 4).

If a visitor asks: "Abhi AI ke ready answers chal rahe hain, game bilkul same hai." Then tell the lead; only Avi raises the cap.

Consent switches

  • Reel on the TV: plays only if the squad ticks "Big screen pe reel chalao" (default off). Never tick it for them.
  • Repost: only reels with "Zenith ise repost kar sakta hai" ticked; an under-18 squad also needs a teacher's or parent's OK. The lead sees the consent list on a staff-only page.
  • WhatsApp (Career Studio): the live-send switch on /career/staff/ is staff-only and stays OFF until the lead says. With it off, forms are saved and PDFs go later; the phone says so.

Filming phones (filming crew, hero capture 26 Oct 14:00 to 17:00)

Pair each filming phone once (code from the lead). Use the fictional demo runs: /jyotish/?c=8&demo=hero, /jyotish/dost.html?c=8&demo=hero, /shark/v2/?c=3&demo=hero, /career/?c=5&demo=hero, /reel/?c=6&demo=hero, /asli/?c=7&demo=hero (counters 2 and 4: only after a go); TV with &capture=1&layout=portrait, R to replay. Hero runs share that counter's TV, so film when the counter is quiet. Minors: hands and screens only.
